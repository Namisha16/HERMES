# MOTION GUIDELINES
Personality: slow, precise, tactile, editorial.
Micro: 180–350ms. UI: 300–500ms. Image reveal: 700–1200ms. Major choreography: 1200–2200ms.
Prefer transform, opacity, scale, translate and masks.
Avoid bounce, excessive spring, particles, rapid zoom and constant movement.
Use subtle parallax only.
Respect prefers-reduced-motion and replace complex choreography with simple fades.
